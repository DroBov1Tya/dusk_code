## Amendments
Amendments replace what they name, in order; a later one wins.

A1. Zone B adds 4.00 instead of 3.00.

A2. Free delivery applies only to zones A and B.

A3. Orders marked fragile add 2.50 after the zone charge.

A4. The percentage in zone C is 25%.

A5. Amendment A1 is withdrawn.

A6. Free delivery does not cover the fragile charge: a free order that is fragile pays 2.50.

A7. Fees are rounded to two places, half up.
