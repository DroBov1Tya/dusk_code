Changed
A goal no longer stops after a set number of rounds: it goes on until it is met, paused or cleared, or a turn fails. To bound it, say so in the goal itself, such as "or stop after 20 turns". When three turns in a row end without using a tool, the goal stops queueing rounds and hands control back with a "goal stalled" note; it stays set and goes on after your next message. The "Goal rounds" setting is gone from the desktop, browser and terminal settings, the terminal goal bar shows "round N", and an old `limits.max_goal_rounds` in config.yml is ignored and dropped on the next save. The core now accepts protocol 38 clients only, since Status and the settings requests dropped the goal-round fields.
