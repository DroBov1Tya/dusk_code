Added
Two guards against work that goes nowhere, on by default. No progress (`limits.no_progress`): steps in a row that change no project file to a new state, show nothing new, read nothing not read before and pass nothing that failed get a note asking where the turn stands at the 12th (`no_progress_note_at`) and a request for a different plan at the 30th (`no_progress_replan_at`), as `NoProgress` and `NoProgressReplan` notices; notes only, and the turn is then a stall candidate for a judge to come. Goal stalls (`limits.goal_stall`): a goal whose last 3 rounds that used tools (`goal_stall_rounds`) left the files its chat changed and its test results (error lines included) as they were pauses for the user, saying what stayed the same; rounds that changed no file and ran no test, wrote where the change ledger does not look (a look at the goal or the tasks is no write), or ran where it never recorded anything are not counted. Looks at a background job, and plain waits once one started in the turn, are not counted as steps without progress; a command whose walk gave up counts as a write, a look at the goal or the tasks does not.
