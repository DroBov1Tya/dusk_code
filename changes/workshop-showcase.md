The Workshop is a storefront in the desktop app, the terminal and the browser: official picks in a Discover carousel, then sections for Official, Verified, Community and Local listings, each with a tier mark (icon, colour and word), version, stars and cover. Filter by category, tier or kind, sort by trust, newest, recently updated or most stars, and "Update all" shows what was updated and what was left with why. A pack opens a page with its skills and their switches; the built-in `duskcode` pack is listed with the local packs. Sources that could not be read get a line each, and installing names the source and tier and warns when another source offers the same name. The browser browses and switches but does not install.
